Overview

Tag & Salesperson Sync

In standard Odoo, Contacts, CRM and Sales each use their own tags and their own Salesperson. The Tag & Salesperson Sync module keeps them in step, so you do not enter the same information again on every record.

It adds two options on the Company. Unify Lead & Contact Tags keeps CRM tags and Contact tags identical and copies a contact's tags to the lead. Use Contact Salesperson on Leads sets the lead's Salesperson from the selected contact.

When a quotation is created from the opportunity with the Sales app, it receives the same tags and Salesperson.

Features

  • Unified tags: Contact, CRM, and Sales tags share the same names. Create or rename a tag in Contacts, and the changes are reflected in CRM and Sales.
  • Duplicate tag names are not allowed across CRM and Contacts.
  • Selecting a contact on a lead or opportunity adds the contact's tags to the lead automatically.
  • Contact salesperson: selecting a contact on a lead or opportunity sets the lead's Salesperson to the contact's Salesperson.
  • With the Sales app installed, New Quotation on the opportunity carries the same tags and Salesperson to the quotation.
  • Both behaviours are controlled by two options on the Company, and each one can be turned on separately.
  • When both options are off, Odoo behaves exactly as before.

Configuration

STEP 1 — ENABLE THE COMPANY OPTIONS

Go to Settings → Companies and open your company. Two options are added:

  • Unify Lead & Contact Tags — shares tags between CRM and Contacts.
  • Use Contact Salesperson on Leads — sets the lead's Salesperson from the contact.
  • Each option can be enabled separately, per company. Leave both off to keep the standard Odoo behaviour.
  • The first time Unify Lead & Contact Tags is enabled, the tags that exist only in CRM or only in Contacts are created on the other side, so both lists match.
STEP 2 — UNIFIED TAGS

With Unify Lead & Contact Tags enabled, create a tag in CRM or in Contacts.

  • The same tag is created on the other side with the same name and colour.
  • Renaming a tag renames the matching tag too.
  • A tag name that already exists in CRM or in Contacts cannot be created again. A message explains why.
STEP 3 — CONTACT TAGS ON CRM

Open a CRM Lead / Opportunity and select a Contact that has tags.

  • The contact's tags are added to the lead's Tags field automatically.
  • Tags already on the lead are kept.
STEP 4 — CONTACT SALESPERSON ON CRM

With Use Contact Salesperson on Leads enabled, select a Contact on a lead or opportunity.

  • The lead's Salesperson is set to the Salesperson defined on the contact.
  • If the contact has no Salesperson, the lead's Salesperson is not changed.
STEP 5 — NEW QUOTATION

On the opportunity, click New Quotation (Sales app installed).

  • The quotation opens with the same Tags as the opportunity.
  • The quotation's Salesperson is the same as on the opportunity.
